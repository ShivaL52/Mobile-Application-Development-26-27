RoomPaau - Research Notes
Project Overview 
This is a mobile application concept to help people to find the rooms for rent in the United Kingdom.
Research Goal 
The goal of this research is to understand : 
Explore existing room-finding platforms.
Identify user needs and common difficulties.
Compare competitor features and user experience.
explore how RoomPaau could provide a useful and user-friendly experience.
Target Users
Students looking for rooms to rent.
People moving to a new area in the UK.
Landlords or authorised providers who want to advertise rooms.
Competitor Research
Platforms to investigate:

OpenRent
